Get to know our coaches better

Coach John

Coach John is an elite No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu specialist and the current #1 ranked athlete in the world under the International Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Federation (IBJJF).

Known for his relentless pace and technical precision, he has earned over 10 IBJJF International Open titles, proving himself as one of the sport’s most dominant competitors.

With 26 years of experience across Boxing and Kickboxing, including a World Championship title in 2015, Coach John’s skills extend far beyond the mat.

His deep combat sports background and championship mindset bring unmatched energy and expertise to every class — inspiring students to train smarter, fight harder, and reach their full potential.

Coach Jamil

Coach Jamil is a world-class martial artist with over 20 years of experience and a black belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.

A fierce competitor and passionate instructor, he’s known for blending technical mastery with an inspiring coaching style that pushes every student to reach their full potential.

A multiple-time IBJJF World Champion, Coach Jamil has proven himself on the global stage, earning respect from athletes and fans alike. He has made history as the first-ever featherweight black belt World Champion, representing Team Lloyd Irvin.

His competitive drive also took him to the 2019 ADCC World Championships — the most prestigious no-gi grappling event in the world — where he battled among the sport’s elite.

Coach Zander

With more than 8 years of dedicated training in Jiu Jitsu and Kickboxing, and 3 years of training in Boxing, Coach Zander has developed a deep passion for the art of combat sports.

He is currently a brown belt in Jiu Jitsu, and is known for his technical precision, calm composure under pressure, and his infectious enthusiam he brings to every class and live sparring session.

Blending his grappling and boxing skills, he continues to refine a well-rounded approach to martial arts. His favorite submissions are the guillotine choke and the rear triangle armbar, techniques that showcase his creativity and understanding of body control and positioning.

Coach Lyndon

Coach Lyndon Yates is an active No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu competitor, currently ranked among the top 15 athletes in the world through the International Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Federation (IBJJF).

Known for his technical precision in leg locks and relentless drive on the mats, Coach Lyndon brings the same competitive spirit to every class — inspiring his students to chase excellence and perform at their best.

Coach Dalash

Coach Dalash is a battle-tested pro with over 20 years of experience in Combat Sambo, wrestling, and MMA.

Hailing originally from Tajikistan-Pamir/Russia, Dalash is not just a fighter — he’s a coach driven by a single mission: building world champions.

His dedication is already showing remarkable results — one of his students has earned an IMMAF World Championship title, eleven have claimed championship belts at the USMMA CHAMP tournament, and four more have placed among the top contenders.

Coach Dan

Coach Dan brings over 17 years of elite coaching experience, having guided more than 20 Virginia State Champions and numerous national titleholders to victory.

A former collegiate standout himself, Coach Dan captured the NCAA National Championship in 2012, showcasing the same drive and discipline he now instills in his athletes.

Blending personal championship experience with a passion for developing the next generation, Coach Dan stands as a true leader in the wrestling community — dedicated to helping every student achieve greatness on and off the mat.
