ADULTS JIU JITSU
Tailored for practitioners of all experience levels — from complete beginners to seasoned grapplers — our program focuses on practical self-defense, building strength and endurance, and developing mental discipline.

Classes are available in both Gi and No-Gi formats, giving students the opportunity to master the art from every angle.

Whether your goal is competition, self-improvement, or overall wellness, you’ll find a supportive and motivating environment that challenges you to grow with every session.



TEENS JIU JITSU (14+)
Designed for students ages 14 and up, this program blends self-defense training, physical conditioning, and personal development into one powerful experience.

Whether you’re stepping onto the mat for the first time or refining existing skills, classes are structured to support every level of learning. Teens gain resilience, discipline, and confidence under pressure, while discovering the value of perseverance and focus.

For those interested in competition, individualized coaching helps each student prepare and progress with confidence.



KIDS JIU JITSU (7 - 13)
A fun and dynamic program designed for kids ages 7 to 13, helping them build confidence, discipline, and real self-defense skills.

Our curriculum focuses on respect, teamwork, and physical fitness, creating a safe, structured, and encouraging environment where young students can thrive—both on and off the mats.



CHILDRENS JIU JITSU (3 - 6)
Designed for children ages 3 to 5, this program helps little ones build coordination, focus, and confidence through fun, interactive activities.

Each class uses games and simple movement exercises to gently introduce the fundamentals of Jiu Jitsu in a safe, playful, and encouraging environment—perfect for young beginners discovering the joy of movement and learning.





INTRO TO MMA
The Intro to MMA Program is designed for beginners ready to step confidently into the world of mixed martial arts.

Students build a well-rounded foundation in striking, grappling, and takedown techniques, while developing real-world self-defense skills, conditioning, and fight strategies in a structured, supportive environment.

This program is the perfect starting point for anyone looking to train like a fighter, build strength, and gain confidence—both inside and outside the gym.



YOUTH MMA (8 - 13)
Our Youth MMA Program is designed for students ages 8 to 13 who want to build confidence, strength, and focus while learning real martial arts skills.

This program introduces young athletes to the fundamentals of striking, grappling, and takedowns in a safe, structured, and supportive environment. Each class focuses on improving coordination, discipline, and resilience, helping students grow both on and off the mats.

Perfect for beginners and experienced students alike, the Youth MMA Program encourages teamwork, respect, and the drive to achieve personal goals—skills that last a lifetime.



ADULTS KICKBOXING
Adult Kickboxing Program delivers high-energy workouts that build strength, stamina, and real self-defense skills.

Each class blends the best of Muay Thai and Western Kickboxing techniques, helping you sharpen your striking, improve your conditioning, and boost your confidence.

Designed for all fitness and experience levels, our program offers a supportive and motivating atmosphere where beginners and seasoned athletes train side by side.



KIDS MUAY THAI (8 - 13)
Proudly presenting Kids Muay Thai program, specifically designed for Kids Ages 8 to 13 to help young athletes build striking skills, fitness, and self-confidence.

Program focuses on building strong striking fundamentals, boosting fitness, and instilling confidence and discipline.
