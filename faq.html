The main difference between Gi and No-Gi Jiu Jitsu comes down to clothing and grip style. In Gi Jiu Jitsu, practitioners wear a traditional uniform (called a Gi), which allows for gripping the fabric to control opponents and execute techniques. In No-Gi Jiu Jitsu, practitioners wear athletic gear like rash guards and shorts, focusing on movements and grips that don’t depend on the fabric. Both styles build essential Jiu Jitsu skills — Gi training sharpens grip strength and technical precision, while No-Gi emphasizes speed, timing, and fluid transitions. Each offers unique benefits and is an excellent way to grow as a practitioner.
